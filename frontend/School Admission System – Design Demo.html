From first click to confirmed seat

Four steps, all on one screen.

Enter your ID

Type the 17-digit student registration number at the top of the page. No account needed.

Apply for a class

Pick a class or shift. A seat is put on hold for you at once.

Pay the fee in time

A live countdown shows how long you have. Payment is simulated in this version.

Seat confirmed

Your admission is final and shows in My Applications, even after you close the browser.

Built so nobody loses a seat unfairly

Never overbooked

Held plus confirmed seats can never go above the total, even when thousands apply in the same second.

Unpaid holds return automatically

If time runs out, the seat goes back to the pool for the next family. Nobody has to press anything.

Live numbers

Total, held, confirmed and available seats refresh by themselves, with no page reload.

Double-click safe

Pressing Pay twice still counts as one admission.

Questions families ask

Do I need to create an account?

No. Your 17-digit registration number is your identity.

What if my hold expires?

The seat is released. You can apply again if seats are still available.

Can I apply to more than one class?

Yes, one active application per class. Morning and Day shifts are separate classes.

Ready to claim a seat?

Have your 17-digit registration number ready. The portal opens in one tap.
